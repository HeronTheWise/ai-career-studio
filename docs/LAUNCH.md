# Global launch and commercial strategy

## Positioning
A career-document studio for job seekers who want clear, tailored, editable application materials without exaggeration. Avoid unsupported claims such as “guaranteed interview”, “100% ATS pass”, or “beat every ATS”.

## Initial customer segments
Test one segment at a time: (1) early-career professionals, (2) mid-career job switchers, (3) tech/product roles, and (4) international applicants. Do not market all segments equally until conversion data identifies a winner.

## Price hypotheses (USD; validate with actual buyers)
- Starter: $9 one-time, one resume and PDF/DOCX.
- Career Pack: $19 one-time, resume + cover letter + LinkedIn text + tailored version.
- Career Pro: $39 one-time, expanded tailored versions and interview prep.
- Later test subscription only if users have ongoing job-search value; explain renewal and cancellation clearly.

Localize price, currency, tax display and payment methods by country. Confirm merchant-of-record / tax obligations with a qualified provider before global checkout.

## 30-day validation plan
Week 1: Interview 8–12 job seekers; collect anonymized examples of pain points; test three positioning statements and a landing page.
Week 2: Recruit 20–30 design partners; measure completion rate, document quality ratings, time-to-first-export and factual correction rate.
Week 3: Run a small paid pilot; test two price points and one bundle; record payment conversion, refund requests, support minutes and AI cost/order.
Week 4: Review cohort results; improve the biggest funnel drop-off; decide whether to scale acquisition, change positioning, or pause.

## Channel order
1. Helpful search-led pages and examples for role-specific resume guidance.
2. Partnerships with career coaches, bootcamps, alumni groups and university career offices.
3. Useful LinkedIn content and short before/after examples with consent and anonymization.
4. Referral credit only after fraud and margin checks.
5. Paid search/social after landing-page conversion and contribution margin are measured.

## Core metrics
- Activation: profile completion and first document exported.
- Quality: user rating, factual correction rate, template overflow defects.
- Funnel: landing page → start → completed profile → checkout → paid → export.
- Economics: net revenue/order, AI + rendering cost/order, acquisition cost, refund rate and contribution margin.
- Reliability: generation success, p95 generation time, failed exports and support tickets/order.

## Scale gates (initial hypotheses, not industry benchmarks)
Before materially increasing ad spend, target a high successful-export rate, low factual correction rate, manageable refunds and positive contribution margin after acquisition. Set numerical gates after the pilot baseline rather than treating generic benchmarks as guarantees.
